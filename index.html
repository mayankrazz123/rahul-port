Passionate 3D Artist Specializing in High-End Asset Creation

With over 2+ years of professional experience in the 3D industry, I specialize in creating high-quality models and textures for motion graphics, animation projects, and product visualization. My expertise lies in hard-surface modeling, basic organic sculpting, lighting, and PBR texturing workflows.

I've collaborated with creative studios and freelance clients, delivering production-ready assets that meet professional quality standards. From concept to final render, I ensure every detail and polygon supports clean visuals and efficient workflow.

My work focuses on three core areas: 3D Modeling & Texturing (optimized and realistic assets), Motion Graphics (dynamic and engaging visuals), and Product Visualization (clean commercial renders).
